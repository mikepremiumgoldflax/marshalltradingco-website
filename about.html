Our Story

Decades of Frontier Commerce

The Marshall Trading Company

★

Marshall Trading Company is an established commodity trading firm headquartered in Saskatchewan, Canada — the heart of North America's grain belt. For decades, we have specialized in the sourcing, trading, and supply of premium flax seed, organic brown flax, and specialty grains to leading manufacturers across the continent.

Our story is one of relationships. From the farmers who grow our commodities to the manufacturers who transform them into products that nourish millions, every link in our supply chain is built on trust, transparency, and a handshake that means something.

We are not a faceless brokerage. We are traders who know our growers by name, who visit the fields, who understand the seasons, and who stand behind every bushel we deliver.

"Where a handshake still means something."

What Sets Us Apart

  • Decades of Market Knowledge — Deep expertise in international flax and specialty grain markets, with sourcing networks spanning Argentina and the Canadian prairies.
  • Relationship-Driven Trading — Long-term supply partnerships with leading North American health food manufacturers, built on reliability and mutual respect.
  • Quality Without Compromise — Rigorous standards for every commodity we handle, from organic certifications to purity testing.
  • International Reach, Local Roots — Based in Saskatchewan with global sourcing capabilities, we bridge continents while staying grounded in prairie values.

Our Approach

In an industry increasingly dominated by impersonal commodity exchanges and algorithmic trading, Marshall Trading Company remains committed to the old way of doing business — direct relationships, personal accountability, and a genuine understanding of both ends of the supply chain.

We source from growers we know and trust. We supply manufacturers whose standards match our own. And we ensure that every transaction reflects the integrity that has defined our firm since day one.

"We don't just move commodities. We build supply chains that last generations."
